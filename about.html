ABOUT

Dohyung Chung Designing Compassionate Patient Experiences

Hi, I'm Dohyung. I enjoy creating meaningful change through small actions that make a big impact. I studied Product Design and Computer Science at Handong Global University in South Korea and am now based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

After several years of working as a designer, the COVID-19 pandemic inspired me to pursue a new path in healthcare. I recently graduated from the College of Marin with an Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) and am currently pursuing my BSN at Sonoma State University.

During nursing school, I developed a particular interest in Medical-Surgical and Critical Care nursing, where I enjoy combining clinical problem-solving, continuous learning, and compassionate patient care in dynamic healthcare environments.

Whether through design or nursing, I am passionate about improving people's experiences and creating meaningful moments for those I work with and care for. Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you enjoy exploring my portfolio.
